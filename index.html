About

I am a self-taught developer working mainly in Luau and Python. Over the past two years I have built two complete Roblox games, a Python desktop application currently in testing with external users, and a set of Python tools that integrate with a live game API.

My work covers gameplay systems, client–server architecture, data persistence, and interface design. I also model, rig, and export 3D assets in Blender for use in Roblox.

I am studying geography with a focus on geospatial analysis, working toward a role in GIS and spatial data. I am building the technical side alongside my coursework, primarily QGIS, GeoPandas, and remote sensing.
